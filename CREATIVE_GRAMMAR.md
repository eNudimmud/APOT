# λP⊙T — the human frame

Production direction, 4 October 2026. Cultural influences belong inside our original creations. The public site does not name the works or send visitors to a reference catalogue. An image must remain compelling even when a viewer does not recognise an allusion.

## Shared language

One person, one physical action, one perceptible consequence. Use the existing midnight workbench, ivory paper, graphite, dark glass, metal and real instruments. Natural skin and plausible reflections matter. A repeated framing or gesture establishes a pattern; one small change gives the viewer a reason to watch again.

Shoot with a restrained 35 mm perspective for people and a macro perspective for contact, graphite and strings. Prefer a fixed camera or a slow deliberate movement. Warm practical light and cool daylight produce the navy/ivory contrast. Leave the sound of the room audible. Final copy is brief English set outside the picture, using our real wordmark and typography.

Identity survives a change of surface. A choice interrupts repetition. Intention leaves a trace. A gesture reaches another person. These four relationships supply the cultural allusions without character replicas, imaginary equipment, costumes or a narrated explanation.
